APPLE INC. - CONDENSED CONSOLIDATED STATEMENTS OF OPERATIONS (UNAUDITED)
Three Months Ended December 28, 2024 (Q1 FY25) - Published: January 30, 2025

Total net sales for the quarter reached $124.3 billion, compared to $119.6 billion in the year-ago quarter.
Products revenue was $94.2 billion, driven by strong iPhone 16 family demand.
Services revenue reached an all-time record of $30.1 billion, up 14% year-over-year.
Total gross margin expanded to 46.9%, compared to 45.9% in the prior year quarter.
Operating income was $42.8 billion, representing an operating margin of 34.4%.
CEO Commentary: 'Our record revenue of $124.3 billion was driven by unprecedented customer engagement with Apple Intelligence and strong international expansion.'
